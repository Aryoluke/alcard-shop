Good questions

Questions people ask before ordering

If yours isn't here, WhatsApp us — a human replies the same day.

Is the card itself NFC?

No — the card is a plain card, and the NFC chip is a sticker attached to it. Tap the sticker. Same result, a fraction of the price of an NFC-embedded card.

What does the card feel like? What's it made of?

Choose your card: PVC (crisp, matte, like a hotel key card) or metal (slim stainless steel, heavier and premium-feeling). The sticker is vinyl with a slight texture, and sticks to any flat surface. Most people take the card + sticker combo for everyday use.

Is it waterproof? Will it survive my wallet?

Yes. PVC, metal and the sticker itself shrug off water, sweat and daily wallet abuse. The chip is sealed under the sticker — the same tech as a contactless bank card.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes — tap the top of an iPhone, the back of an Android. Any phone with NFC reads it. No app needed on either end.

Do the people I give it to need an app?

No. Their phone opens your profile page in the browser automatically — contact details, socials and Save Contact. That's the whole point.

What if I change my number or job?

Message us and we update your profile page — free, forever. Your card never goes out of date, unlike paper cards.

What do I need to send you to set up my page?

Just your name and how you want to be reached — phone, email, WhatsApp, website, Instagram, LinkedIn, whatever you use. Send a photo and we'll put it on the page too. Takes about 5 minutes and we do all the work.

Why is it so much cheaper than the big brands?

NFC chips cost pennies, your profile is hosted on free infrastructure, and setup is largely automated. The big brands charge £30+ for the card and then a subscription on top for the same simple thing. We charge one small price, and extras at near cost.

Can I see WHO tapped my card?

No — and that's deliberate. Every tap pings your phone with device type, city, what they opened and how long they stayed, but never a name or identity. Anonymous and GDPR-friendly.

Do I get a QR code?

Yes — every order includes a digital QR code for your profile page. Show it on your screen, drop it in your email signature, or print it yourself. It's the backup if someone's phone can't tap.

Why NFC instead of just a QR code?

QR codes need the camera open, a frame around the code, and a moment to scan — and plenty of people ignore them. NFC works instantly: tap the top of an iPhone or the back of an Android and your page opens on its own. No app, no camera, no fuss. (You still get the QR code as a backup.)

What is a "custom domain" add-on?

For +£5 one-time on Basic or Standard, your profile page gets a clean personalised URL (for example yourname.alcard.uk) instead of a long standard one. Easier to say out loud, easier to remember, looks more serious. Included free in Premium.

How fast is delivery?

UK: 3–4 days from order to doorstep. Overseas: about 7 days. Need it faster? Say so on the order and we'll do our best.

Can I order for my whole team or business?

Yes. 5+ cards get 10% off, and for companies we do fully custom runs — your branding on the cards, a consistent page per team member, and team tap analytics. Message us for a quote.

How do I pay?

After you send your order we confirm it and send payment details — card, bank transfer or PayPal. No subscription, no recurring charges, ever.
