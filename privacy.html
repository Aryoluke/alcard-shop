Privacy Policy

Alcard · Last updated: August 2026 · Plain English, no jargon.

Who we are

We sell NFC business cards. Each card links to a personal profile page. Contact: aryanjigar.jmehta@gmail.com.

This website (the shop)

This shop collects nothing. No cookies, no tracking, no analytics. Ordering happens by opening WhatsApp or email with a pre-written message — we only see what you choose to send us.

Customer profile pages (the pages your card opens)

So that card owners can see their card is being used, each profile page collects the following anonymous information about visits:

  • Device type (e.g. iPhone, Android phone, desktop)
  • Operating system and browser
  • Country and city (from your internet connection's IP address)
  • Which buttons you tapped and how long you looked at the page

Your IP address itself is not stored or shared — it is used once, in the moment, to look up the country. None of this can identify you personally.

What we do NOT collect

  • No cookies
  • No device fingerprinting
  • No saved autofill data, passwords or form details
  • No photos or files from your device

Why we collect it

Card owners pay for their card partly to know it works. "Your card was tapped 14 times this month, mostly iPhones in London" is only possible with basic visit statistics. Nothing is sold to anyone, ever.

Your rights

You don't need to do anything — the data is anonymous. If you have any question about this policy, email us and we'll answer within 48 hours.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top will be updated.
